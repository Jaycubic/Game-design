# Clearwater Run — Rule Book

*One crew, one river, against the pollution.* 1–4 players, each on their own device, all on the same
team. A run lasts 3 minutes.

---

## 1. Getting into a run

1. Sign in and press **PLAY**. The game opens a room and shows a six-character **invitation code**.
2. Friends press **Join** on their own devices and type the code. Up to 4 players, all in one crew.
3. Everyone presses **Ready**, and the host starts the run.

**Practice solo** starts a run with just you. It pays half Seeds. **∞ Endless** has no clock (§11): play it alone
from the hub, or with your crew from the room (the host presses ∞ Endless).

The **river chip** above PLAY chooses the river (§4); in a crew room the host's choice counts.

**Tutorial** is a 60-second course that teaches each control in turn. You can skip it at any time, and it pays
nothing.

## 2. One crew against the pollution

Everyone in the room is **one crew**. You share one score, one River Health bar and one result, and you play
together against the pollution. A grumpy brown smog cloud sulks over every open sewage pipe until you plug it.

- **You saved the river** when the run ends with the river **revived** (River Health 60 or more).
- **the river chokes** if River Health ever reaches 0 (the river **chokes**).

## 3. Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad | Phone / tablet |
|---|---|---|---|
| Steer left / right | ◀ ▶ or A / D | D-pad or left stick | ◀ ▶ buttons, or swipe left / right |
| Hop | ▲, W or Space | A | ▲ button, or swipe up |
| Action (cut weed or nets, start plugging) | ▼, S or E | B or X | ✂ button, or swipe down |
| Send your pipe number / type a number lock | keys 1–9 | hold RT + A/B/X/Y/LB/RB (the 1st–6th number on screen) | tap your big number, or the lock's numbers |
| Vote during a rule change | keys 1–3 | A / B / X | tap a card |

Your boat moves forward on its own. The letter keys can be rebound in **Settings**; the arrow keys always work.

**Pause and quit:** tap the **⏸ Pause** button in the top-left corner (or press **Esc** or **P** on a computer).
The menu has **Resume** and **Quit run** (Quit asks once more). Playing alone, the river really stops until you resume
(it resumes by itself after 10 minutes, and the game pauses when you switch away from the tab). In a crew run
**only your boat waits**: it stops, nothing can hit it, and the others keep racing; your number fills in by itself so
their pipe locks still work. Press Resume and your boat rejoins just behind the crew, protected for a moment. Quitting
takes you back to the hub; the others finish the run without you (even one player alone can still fix every pipe), and
votes no longer wait for you. **Refreshing the page (or closing the tab) counts as quitting**: you land back on the
hub, not in the old run. The tutorial has its own **Skip tutorial** button instead.

**On-screen controls** (Settings → Controls), as in most mobile games:

| Choice | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Buttons + swipes** | Thumb buttons and swipes on the river. The default on phones and tablets. |
| **Buttons only** | Thumb buttons; swipes are ignored. |
| **Swipes only** | No thumb buttons: swipe ← → to steer, ↑ to hop, ↓ to cut or plug. |
| **Off** | Keyboard or gamepad only. The default on PCs; a touch-screen laptop can switch the others on. |

With buttons you can also pick the **button size** (small, medium, large) and a **left-handed** layout (steering on
the right, hop and cut on the left). There are no number buttons: you tap the big number itself, or the numbers
on a lock. Keyboard and gamepad keep working whatever you choose.

## 4. The river

The river widens with the number of boats, and carries more plastic and more obstacles when it does:

| Boats | Lanes |
|---|---|
| 1 | 3 |
| 2 | 4 |
| 3–4 | 6 |

Boats start spread across the river.

| Thing | What it does | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Plastic** (bottles, bags) | Scores 1 point for your crew, fills your boost and heals the river a little. Plastic you miss hurts the river. | Drive into it. |
| **Weed mat** | Slows you for a second. | Press **Action** just before it to cut it. Each cut greens a bank: +5 crew score and the river heals. |
| **Log** | Capsizes you: you stop for about a second and spill 3 plastic, which floats just ahead for anyone to net. Then you're safe from obstacles for 1 second. A red shadow under a log in your lane means hop now. | **Hop** over it. |
| **Debris wave** (every 30 s) | Covers every lane but one (two gaps on a 6-lane river) and capsizes you. The gap lanes glow. | Steer to a gap, or hop. |
| **Sewage pipe** (on a bank, with a smog cloud above it) | Pours sewage that drains River Health every second until it is plugged. | Pull up beside it in the outermost lane on its side, press **Action**, and fill its lock with your crew (§5). |

**Other boats:** if you run into the back of a boat in your lane, you slow down for a moment. Hop or change lanes
to pass.

**Boost:** collecting plastic fills your boost meter. When it is full, your boat surges at 1.5× speed for
2.5 seconds, and weed can't slow you while you boost.

### Rivers

Pick the river from the river button above **PLAY** (in a crew, the host picks it in the lobby). Each river adds one
new thing on top of everything above.

| River | What's new | Unlocks at |
|---|---|---|
| **Town Canal** | Everything above. | Start |
| **Mangrove Maze** | **Ghost nets**: old fishing nets across two lanes. They slow you like weed. Cut one with **Action** twice (or a crewmate cuts once too) to free the 4 plastic trapped inside. | 3 runs or 3 ★ |
| **Rapids Gorge** | **Currents** (white arrows) push you one lane sideways unless you hop over them. **Rocks** capsize you even if you hop: steer round them. | 8 runs or 7 ★ |

## 5. Plugging a pipe: the number lock

**Finding a pipe.** Open pipes stand out on the bank: a big pipe, a brown plume in the water, a grumpy smog cloud above it,
and a floating 🔧 marker with the distance. A card at the top of the screen tells you which bank it's on, how far
away it is, and what to do next. With friends, the marker also suggests whose turn it is to fix one ("Asha's turn?",
whoever has fixed the fewest so far). It's only a hint: anyone can fix any pipe.

### With a crew (2–4 players): see your number, press it

1. **Your number.** As the crew nears a pipe (about 90 m), **each player gets a big number from 1 to 9** on their
   own screen ("YOUR NUMBER 7"). Every player gets a different number. It stays up for **8 seconds** and **blinks**
   as it is about to disappear. Nothing to remember.
2. **Press it.** Press that number (or tap the big number on a phone). That's you **helping fix the pipe**: the
   game puts it on the crew's **board** for whoever fixes the pipe, and your card turns into a calm "✔ Your 7 is in".
   A wrong number does nothing; try again. **Every player must press**: the pipe can't be fixed without all the numbers.
3. **Fix.** A player beside the pipe presses **Action**. Their boat stops and the **lock** opens. It shows the
   board: **every number already sent, in the order it was sent**, the fixer's own, and "…" for anyone still missing. Anyone who hasn't pressed yet gets
   their number back on screen, blinking, with "Your crew needs it!" — until they press or the lock closes.
4. **Type them in.** The fixer types the numbers on the board (keys 1–9, or tap them) **in any order**; the easy way
   is just as they appear (board 5 · 6 · 7 → type 5, 6, 7). The lock opens only when **every player's number** is in:
   2 players need both numbers, 3 players all three, 4 players all four. A number that isn't on the board (yet) is
   ignored, with no penalty. The fixer has **12 seconds**.
5. **Fixed!** The boat stays put 1 more second while the crew member hammers the pipe shut. The smog cloud over that pipe
   is gone, the river heals by 10, and your crew's **multiplier** goes up for the rest of the run (×1.5, ×2… next to
   your score).
6. **Out of time:** **the river takes a hit**, River Health −5, the pipe keeps pouring, and everyone gets **new
   numbers** for the next try.

**Skip.** A fixer can leave the lock at any time by pressing **▲ (hop)**, with no penalty. This is how a crew that
all stopped can sail on and leave one fixer behind.

**More than one fixer.** Several players can open a lock at the same pipe. Every sent number shows on all of them,
and the **first** fixer to finish fixes the pipe.

**Catching up.** A boat more than about 25 m behind the front boat sails 15 % faster until it rejoins, so stopping
to fix a pipe never strands anyone.

**One pipe at a time.** Numbers belong to the pipe you're approaching. The next pipe's numbers come once that pipe is
fixed or everyone has passed it.

**Worked example (Ben, You and Asha).**

| Step | You (the fixer) | Ben and Asha |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Your number: **6**. | Ben gets **1**. Asha gets **3**. |
| 2 | Press **6** straight away (or wait: the lock shows it anyway). | Ben presses **1**. Asha is busy dodging a log and doesn't press yet. |
| 3 | Press **Action** beside the pipe. The board shows: Ben **1** · You **6** · Asha … (the lock waits for her) | Asha's **3** comes back, blinking: "Your crew needs it!" She presses **3**. |
| 4 | The board now shows 1 · 6 · 3 (the order they were sent). Type **1, 6, 3** (any order works). | Keep sailing. |
| 5 | Fixed: River +10, every plastic now ×1.5. | Same score boost for everyone. |

**Away players** (paused, disconnected or quit): their number sends itself, so a lock is never impossible.

### Playing alone: three numbers, any order

With no crewmates, the lock shows **three numbers** (for example 7 · 2 · 5). Type all three, **in any order**,
within 8 seconds; they stay on screen the whole time. ▲ skips here too. Running out of time makes the river take a
hit (River Health −5).

## 6. Score and River Health

**Crew score** = plastic × (1 + 0.5 × pipes plugged) + 5 × banks greened

For example, 40 plastic with 2 pipes plugged and 3 banks greened scores 40 × 2 + 15 = 95.

**River Health** starts at 70 out of 100.

| Event | Effect on River Health |
|---|---|
| Plastic collected | +0.5 |
| Weed cut | +1.5 |
| Pipe plugged | +10 |
| Plastic missed | −0.8 |
| Each open pipe | −0.12 every second |
| A pipe left open (every boat rowed 15 m past it without plugging) | −5 once; it stops draining after that |

- **River choked:** if River Health ever reaches 0, the crew's final score is **halved**.
- **River revived:** if the run ends with River Health at 60 or more, the crew gets **+20**.

## 7. The two rule changes

Every run has **exactly two** rule changes, and players choose both. Each one goes through the same steps:

1. a 5-second warning while you keep racing;
2. the run pauses and the choice opens;
3. a full-screen card shows the new rule;
4. a badge stays on the HUD, and you get 3 seconds to adjust before racing resumes.

The controls, the score formula and the win condition never change.

### Rule change 1: Twist (about 1:03 into the run)

Everyone votes within 15 seconds. The option with the most votes wins. If two options tie (for example 1–1 with two
players), a tie goes to the option that got the **first vote** (the result card names who voted first), so the same votes always give the same river on every screen. Only if nobody votes at all does the river pick at random (a visible spin). You can change your vote until everyone has voted; changing it counts as a new, later vote.

| Twist | Upside | Downside |
|---|---|---|
| **Monsoon Surge** | Extra plastic appears. | Debris waves come every 15 s. |
| **Dry Season** | Fewer lanes to watch. | The river loses a third of its lanes (6 → 4, 4 → 3, 3 → 2), and pipes pour twice as fast. |
| **Volunteer Day** | Each plastic counts double. | Weed mats spread. |

Every screen shows the Twist that won: **Monsoon Surge** brings rain and a grey sky, **Dry Season** a hot, pale sky
over mud flats, **Volunteer Day** a bright sky and a burst of confetti. A badge under the timer names it for the rest of the run.

### Rule change 2: Finale (about 2:20 into the run)

The crew **votes** within 10 seconds, like the Twist. The option with the most votes wins; a tie goes to the first
vote, and only no votes at all means a visible spin.

| Finale | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Second Wind** | Every boat's boost fills, and extra plastic flows into your lanes for 20 s. |
| **Clean Sweep** | Every open pipe pours at half rate for the rest of the run. |

The Finale is a crew decision: chase points (Second Wind) or protect the river (Clean Sweep).

## 8. End of the run

- **You saved the river** when the river ends revived (River Health 60 or more). The results say "River revived! You saved it
  together".
- **the river chokes** if River Health reached 0. Otherwise the run is complete and your crew score still counts.
- The results also show how many pipes each player fixed.

**Stars.** Every river has three stars to earn, and each one stays earned:

| Star | Town Canal | Mangrove Maze | Rapids Gorge |
|---|---|---|---|
| ★ Clean river | River Health 60 % or more at the end | same | same |
| ★★ Score | Crew score 120 or more | 150 or more | 180 or more |
| ★★★ River goal | Plug every pipe you pass | Free 3 ghost nets | Finish without capsizing |

The results screen shows the stars from this run (new ones are marked) and your best score on that river. Stars
help unlock the next river.

## 9. Seeds and boats

**Seeds** are the only currency, and they are only earned by playing.

**Seeds per run:**
- Your crew score ÷ 5, up to 60.
- **+20** for a revived river. Only your first 3 wins each day earn this bonus.
- **+10** if the river was revived.
- Practice runs pay half. The tutorial pays nothing.

**Daily reward:** collect once per day (UTC). The track pays 20, 25, 30, 40, 50, 60, then 100 Seeds, and then
repeats. A missed day pauses the track; it never resets it.

**River Road:** rewards for runs completed.

| Runs completed | Reward |
|---|---|
| 1 | 50 Seeds + the Kingfisher boat |
| 3 | 75 Seeds |
| 5 | 100 Seeds |
| 8 | 150 Seeds |
| 10 | 100 Seeds + the River Keeper boat |
| 15 | 300 Seeds |

**Boats.** Each stat runs from 1 to 5, and level 2 is the starter's level.

| Boat | How to get it | Speed | Net | Cutter | Boost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Driftwood (rowboat) | Starter | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Kingfisher (fishing boat) | River Road, 1 run | 2 | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| Coral Skiff | Shop, 120 Seeds | 3 | 2 | 2 | 3 |
| Bluefin | Shop, 120 Seeds | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| Mangrove | Shop, 200 Seeds | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| Night Heron (jet boat) | Shop, 300 Seeds | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 |
| River Keeper (cleanup tug) | River Road, 10 runs | 3 | 5 | 4 | 3 |

Each level above 2 gives:

| Stat | Effect per level above 2 |
|---|---|
| Speed | +4 % top speed |
| Net | +15 % pickup reach. Level 5 also scoops plastic from the next lane. |
| Cutter | +15 % cutting reach |
| Boost | +15 % boost charge per plastic |

The upgrades are deliberately small, so new players can still keep up. Equip a boat in the **Garage**. You can undo
any purchase for a full refund within 10 minutes.

## 10. Daily missions and ranks

**Missions.** Every day (UTC) you get three missions, such as "Collect 60 plastic", "Plug 2 sewage pipes", "Earn a
star on any river", "Fix a pipe yourself" or "Finish a run with the river revived". Progress counts in every run, practice included (not the tutorial). A
mission pays 20–40 Seeds the moment it is done. Open **Missions** in the hub to see today's three.

**Ranks.** Your rank shows how well you've been playing: Rookie, Deckhand, Skipper, Captain, River Guardian. It
goes up as you play well, and it can drop by at most one step in a day. The bar shows how close you are to the next
rank. The results screen shows your missions and rank after every run.

## 11. Endless

**∞ Endless** is a run with no clock on any river you have unlocked. Start it alone from the hub (next to Practice
solo), or with your crew from the room (the host presses ∞ Endless):
- **Alone:** one capsize ends the run. **With a crew:** you share **3 lives** (❤️❤️❤️ under the score); every capsize
  uses one, and the run ends when they're gone or the river chokes.
- The boats speed up by 2 % every 15 seconds.
- The rules still change exactly twice, and you choose: a Twist at 1:00 and a Finale at 2:30.
- Pipes use the number lock (crew or alone).
- **Score = metres travelled + 5 per plastic collected.** Each river keeps your best Endless score.
- It pays Seeds like a practice run and counts towards daily missions. It earns no stars.

## 12. Disconnections

If your connection drops, the game reconnects automatically and you keep your seat. During a run your boat keeps
moving forward on its own until you return.

## 13. Fair play

| Possible loophole | How the rules stop it |
|---|---|
| The crew ignores the river and only farms plastic | A choked river halves the score. |
| Nobody pressing their number | The lock can't open without a crewmate's number; if it runs out of time the river takes a hit for the whole crew. Your number blinks until you press it. |
| One player fixes every pipe | The marker suggests whoever has fixed the fewest, and the results show each player's pipes. |
| Stopping to fix leaves you stranded | The catch-up current brings you back to the crew. |
| A rule change is random or forced on players | Both changes are chosen by players. Only a tie, or no choice, spins. |
| Farming wins for Seeds | The win bonus counts only 3 wins a day. Practice pays half. The tutorial pays nothing. |
| Paying to win | There is no real money, and upgrades are capped and modest. |
| A regretted purchase | Undo within 10 minutes for a full refund. |
| Missing a day | The daily track pauses; it never resets. |
| Grinding missions | Each mission pays once, and there are only three a day. |
| One bad day wiping out a rank | A rank drops at most one step per day. |
